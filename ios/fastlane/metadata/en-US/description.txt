FruitySelia turns paper, photos, and PDFs into clean documents without sending their contents to a developer-operated server.

CAPTURE AND IMPORT
• Scan one or more pages with Apple's document camera.
• Import images or PDFs from Photos and Files.
• Reorder pages and review every result before export.

EDIT ON DEVICE
• Crop, rotate, filter, and adjust pages.
• Clean up whiteboards and marks manually or with Smart Cleanup.
• Permanently flatten secure redactions into exported pixels.
• Add reusable visual signatures and stamps.

FIND AND EXPORT
• Recognize text and barcodes on device.
• Use Live Text in the full-screen viewer.
• Create searchable PDFs or JPEG and PNG images.
• Choose original quality, a target PDF size, or a custom size target.
• Save to Files or Photos, share, or print with system controls.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No FruitySelia account, ads, subscription, analytics, or cloud document library.
• Document processing uses Apple frameworks on your device.
• Recent holds up to eight disposable working copies and can be protected with device authentication.
• Saved files remain under your control in Files, Photos, or a provider you select.

FruitySelia supports iPhone and iPad. A scan can contain up to 20 pages. Visual signatures are document marks and are not cryptographic digital signatures.
