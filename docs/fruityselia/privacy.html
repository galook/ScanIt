FruitySelia Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-22

This policy covers the FruitySelia app for iPhone and iPad. Matěj Teplý publishes the app and handles support at majkeylab@gmail.com. Do not send private documents or credentials.

Short version: FruitySelia has no account, advertising, analytics, tracking, subscription, or developer-operated document server. It does not collect data from the app. Document processing runs on your device.

Documents and recognition

Pages you scan or import, OCR text, barcodes, edits, redactions, signatures, stamps, and generated files are processed with Apple frameworks on your device. FruitySelia does not transmit this content to the publisher. Secure redaction creates flattened pixels and excludes intersecting recognition data from searchable PDF export.

Local storage and deletion

Recent scans are temporary working copies in app-private storage, limited to eight completed scans. iOS may remove cache data. Deleting a Recent scan removes that local working copy. Reusable signature and stamp templates are stored separately in app-private storage until you delete them or remove the app. Private app files are excluded from backup and use iOS file protection where supported.

Exports saved to Files, Photos, or a selected file provider remain there until you delete them. Removing FruitySelia does not delete copies stored outside the app. A cloud-backed provider or Photos configuration may upload files under that provider's privacy terms.

Permissions

  • Camera: used to scan documents after you start or accept document capture.
  • Photos: used only after you import, save, inspect, or delete a Photos item through an explicit app action.
  • Face ID or device authentication: optional, off by default, and used to protect Recent scans. FruitySelia does not receive biometric data.
  • Files and providers: access is limited to locations or files you select through system pickers.

Sharing, printing, and external destinations

FruitySelia sends a document to another app, print service, Photos, Files, or a file provider only after you choose that action and destination. The selected service processes the copy under its own terms. Review the destination before sending sensitive material.

Data collection and tracking

The iOS app contains no network client, analytics SDK, advertising SDK, tracking code, account service, or third-party SDK. It does not collect app data and does not track users across apps or websites.

Support

If you email support, the publisher receives the information you include. It is used only to answer and investigate the request and is deleted within 90 days after the case closes, unless an unresolved dispute or legal duty requires longer retention. A case closes when resolved or after 90 days without a reply.

Children

FruitySelia is a general document utility and is not directed to children. It has no account, social, advertising, or public-content features.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated before the app materially changes its data handling. The effective date identifies the current version. Questions and applicable privacy-rights requests may be sent to majkeylab@gmail.com.
