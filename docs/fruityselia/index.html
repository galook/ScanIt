iPhone and iPad

Scan. Edit. Keep it local.

Capture or import documents, recognize text and barcodes, redact sensitive details, add visual signatures, and export searchable PDFs or images.

Document tools without an account.

Native capture

Scan multiple pages with Apple's document camera, or import images and PDFs from Photos and Files.

Local recognition

OCR, Live Text, and barcode detection run with Apple frameworks on your device.

Secure redaction

Redacted areas are flattened into exported pixels and removed from the searchable text layer.

Flexible export

Create searchable PDFs, JPEGs, or PNGs, then save, share, print, or choose a target PDF size.

Visual marks

Add reusable handwritten signatures and stamps. These are visual document marks, not cryptographic signatures.

Private by design

No FruitySelia account, advertising, analytics, subscription, or developer-operated document cloud.
