FruitySelia Support

For help, email majkeylab@gmail.com. Never attach private documents, credentials, payment information, or unredacted scans.

Before contacting support

  • Include the FruitySelia version, iOS or iPadOS version, and device model.
  • Describe the action, expected result, and actual result without including sensitive document content.
  • For Files issues, name the storage provider but do not send its credentials.

Local data

FruitySelia cannot retrieve your scans from a server. Recent scans are disposable local working copies. Saved exports remain in the Files, Photos, or provider destination you selected.

Response and retention

Support email is used only to answer and investigate the request. Correspondence is deleted within 90 days after a case closes unless a longer period is required for an unresolved dispute or legal duty.
